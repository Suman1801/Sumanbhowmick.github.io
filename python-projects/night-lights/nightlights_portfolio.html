Overview

This project uses VIIRS-DNB satellite imagery from Google Earth Engine to animate India’s night-light radiance every month from 2015 to 2025 132 frames of electrification history. The images are processed using log-scaling, noise masking and temporal smoothing standard techniques in remote-sensing research to reveal both the blazing metros and the faint glow of newly electrified villages.

Colour scale radiance intensity

No light (dark rural) Faint glow (small town) Bright (city) Intense (metro core)
Why log scaling? Raw night-light radiance is highly skewed Mumbai emits ~10,000× more light than a small village. Without transformation, cities dominate and rural electrification is invisible. Applying log(1+x) compresses bright pixels and amplifies faint ones, revealing the full story.

💡 Key Insights

Saubhagya Scheme (2017 2018)

The most visually dramatic change in the animation. Watch eastern UP, Bihar and Odisha historically the darkest regions brighten month by month through 2017 18 as the government’s 100% household electrification drive reaches remote villages.

COVID Lockdown (March May 2020)

One of the clearest satellite signatures of the pandemic. Industrial zones around Surat, Pune and Chennai dim noticeably in March 2020 as factories shut. The recovery from June 2020 onwards is equally visible a rare natural experiment in human activity.

Highway Corridors Emerge

By 2019 2022 the Delhi Mumbai Industrial Corridor and the Chennai Bengaluru highway appear as bright linear features in the animation. These infrastructure investments are legible from space before they register in economic statistics.

Monsoon Dimming (Jun Sep)

Every year shows a subtle seasonal pattern cloud cover during the monsoon months attenuates the satellite signal. This is physical, not economic. Temporal smoothing reduces but does not fully remove this seasonal oscillation, which is itself an interesting data artefact.

🕐 Year by Year

2015
Baseline year. ~300 million people still without electricity. Major metros blaze; eastern India largely dark.
2016
Demonetisation in November. Digital payment infrastructure spreads to semi-urban areas. Slight economic disruption visible.
2017
Saubhagya scheme launched (Sept). Target: every household electrified. Eastern India begins its dramatic brightening.
2018
Peak Saubhagya rollout. Bihar, UP, Odisha and Jharkhand show the fastest month-on-month brightness increase in the dataset.
2019
India officially fully electrified (April 2018 declaration). 2019 is the pre-pandemic brightness peak India’s night-light footprint at its largest.
2020
COVID-19. National lockdown from March 25. Industrial zones dim. Highways go dark. Recovery visible from June as restrictions ease.
2021
Post-COVID rebound. Manufacturing restarts. Urban centres recover rapidly India’s GDP growth fastest in the world by Q2 2021.
2022
Russia-Ukraine war drives energy demand. India’s power consumption at record highs. New industrial corridors under construction.
2023
G20 presidency. Infrastructure investment visible as new nodes of brightness appear in tier-2 cities and logistics hubs.
2024
World’s fastest-growing major economy. Delhi Mumbai corridor, Chennai Bengaluru axis and coastal industrial zones at their brightest.
2025
Latest available data. India’s cumulative night-light brightness index at ~1.4× the 2015 baseline a 40% increase in a decade.
